# Claims & disclosure

## Never say (unless the product data proves it, and even then hedge)

- Absolute: "dijamin", "100%", "pasti", "paling …", "nomor 1", "permanen", "langsung hilang", "dalam 1 hari"
- Health/medical outcomes (sembuh, menghilangkan penyakit, menurunkan berat badan X kg)
- Income promises (dijamin cuan, pasti balik modal)
- Fake scarcity: "kehabisan", "stok terbatas", "sebelum habis", "terakhir" — only if the data says stock is limited
- Invented discounts or prices; quote only the price in the product data

## Say instead

"bisa bantu …", "kerasa lebih …", "buat aku …", "hasil tiap orang bisa beda", "mumpung masih promo" (only with a real promo), "cek keranjang di bawah ya".

## Disclosure

The creator earns affiliate commission → a material connection. Keep the post honest (no fake "beli sendiri" if it was sent free) and follow TikTok's branded-content policy; add #ad / #affiliate when the market requires it.

## Platform hygiene

- Music: royalty-free or TikTok in-app sounds only (copyrighted music gets muted).
- AI-made video: keep provenance watermarks (SynthID); use TikTok's AI-generated content label where required.
- Do not reuse another creator's footage; KaloData videos are references for style only.
