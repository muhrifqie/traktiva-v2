# What best-selling affiliate videos do (measured)

Source: 30 best-selling videos (6 per product, 5 Indonesian TikTok Shop products: 3 shoes, 1 clothing set, 1 gaming mouse), KaloData, Nov 2026. Full data: `docs/edit-style-kalodata.md`. Small sample, skewed to shoes, no losing videos to compare — patterns, not proof.

## Formats

| | Review / ngobrol (18/30) | Estetik / musik (12/30) | Demo cepat (gadget) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Length | 30–68 s (median ~50) | 13–20 s | 30–54 s |
| Audio | Voice from second 0, 165–200 words/min | Music only | Voice + demo sounds |
| Picture | Face + product in hand, 1 take | Product in hand / worn, 1 take | Cuts every ~3 s (13–19 per video), demo results |
| Text | None, or 1–3 word subtitles, small, center-lower (y≈0.66), one word highlighted | 2 small lines at the top for the whole video ("Sepatu cakep poll / Empuk, nyaman di kaki ✨") | 1-word subtitles low (y≈0.78), bold title only in the first 2 s |
| CTA | Spoken + urgency | None on screen | Spoken |

Numbers: one-take (≤1 cut) 18/30 · product visible at frame 0: 30/30 · price badge / CTA sticker on screen: 0/30 · zoom/flash transitions: 0/30 · loudness median −16.7 LUFS · post caption ~33 characters + ~4 hashtags, often a struck price ("1,6 jt❌ 160rb✅").

TikTok guidance (official): hook in the first 3–6 s and related to the product; structure hook → key message → CTA (spoken or text); in-feed ads 21–34 s recommended; urgency (promo, free shipping) helps; keep key elements in the safe zone.

## Hook patterns (first line, 0–4 s)

1. Price anchor — "looknya kayak sepatu jutaan, padahal cuma 160 ribuan" · "harganya bukan 1 juta, bukan 500 ribu…"
2. Stop doing X — "Di rumah jangan pakai daster terus, Bun" · "jangan beli yang KW, ini original"
3. Demo challenge — "kalian bisa gini ga?" (while showing the feature)
4. Trend / social proof — "Ini yang lagi booming dan turun harga guys"

## Closing (spoken CTA)

"cek keranjang kuning" / "link di kiri bawah" + light urgency only when true: "mumpung masih promo", pre-order note. Never a sticker by default.

## Pace

~2.5–3 words per second. A 6 s clip ≈ 15 words; a 30 s one-take ≈ 75–80 words. Silence gaps read as low effort: fit clips to the voice.

## Safe area (no official spec; conservative)

Bottom 25% (33% with TikTok Shop product anchor), right 15–16%, top 9–12%. Keep text and the product out of those margins.
